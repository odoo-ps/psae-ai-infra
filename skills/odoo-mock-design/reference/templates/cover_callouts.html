Interactive mock. Step through the screens with Next / Previous in the bar at the bottom (or the arrow keys). The screens themselves respond too — buttons open modals, tabs switch panels, status pivots advance the form.
Multiple workflows. Switch between them with the Workflow chip at the left of the bar. Each workflow opens with a short overview, and Next flows from the last screen of one workflow into the next.
Variant chips. Some screens carry small pills like Axis:Value in the bar. Click one to pivot the same screen across different states without leaving it.
Numbered markers. Click any numbered i to read what the highlighted element does. Use the Annotations chip at the right of the bar to hide them for a clean view.
Field help. Hover the ? next to a field label to see what the field stores and how it behaves — the same help text shipped with the module.
